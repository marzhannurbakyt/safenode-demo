SafeNode

Autonomous emergency detection for people who may be unable to call for help.

Functional Prototype Kazakhstan · 2026 Mining · Oil & Gas · Construction
01 · Problem

When a worker cannot call for help.

Falls

A sudden event can leave a worker unable to interact with a device.

Impacts

A severe impact may prevent a worker from initiating an alert.

Loss of reaction

Consciousness or responsiveness can be lost before help is requested.

Prolonged immobility

Danger can remain invisible when nobody is watching the worker directly.

Most emergency workflows depend on the worker initiating the signal. SafeNode addresses the gap when the worker cannot.
02 · Solution

Detect the event. Check the reaction. Trigger the alert.

SensePhysical signals
AnalyzeDetect abnormal patterns
VerifyCheck user reaction
AlertEmergency signal

SafeNode is designed to add an autonomous detection layer to existing industrial safety systems — without replacing them.

03 · Technology

Physical AI, built around the real world.

01

Physical sensing

Motion and contextual signals from a wearable device.

02

Embedded intelligence

Local analysis of abnormal movement and potentially dangerous events.

03

Autonomous escalation

Reaction verification followed by an emergency alert when appropriate.

Functional prototype

Current prototype includes a wearable device, motion sensing, abnormal-motion detection, potential emergency-event detection, reaction checking and emergency alert transmission.

Current testing: fall, prolonged immobility and no-response scenarios.
